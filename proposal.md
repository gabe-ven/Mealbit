# MyFitnessPal Clone Proposal

## App
I plan to clone MyFitnessPal, which is a nutrition app that allows people to keep track of the calories and macronutrients they eat throughout the day.

I chose MyFitnessPal because the app can sometimes feel cluttered and overwhelming. I want to simplify the experience and focus mainly on logging a meal and quickly seeing where you are for the day.

## Fundamental Problem
The fundamental problem that MyFitnessPal tries to solve is helping people keep track of what they eat.

It provides an easy way to remember how many calories, grams of protein, and other macronutrients you consume throughout the day. It gives users one place to record their meals and see their progress toward a daily goal.

## What I'll Keep
I plan to keep the main purpose of the app, which is food logging.

Users will be able to:

- Log meals under breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- Enter how much food they ate
- Track daily calories
- Track protein, fat, and carbohydrates
- View a simple daily summary of their progress

I want to keep the daily summary because that is one of the main things people want to see when tracking their meals.

## What I'll Change
I would remove features that are not necessary for the core experience and make the app feel more complicated than it needs to be.

This would include things like:

- Social features
- Recipes
- Workout tracking
- Coaching
- Other extra features unrelated to basic food logging

I want my version to be very simple and only focus on the food logging system, which is the main purpose of the app. This way, users will not have to go through unnecessary screens or features that make the app feel overwhelming.
